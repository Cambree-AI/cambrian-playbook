Does it close deals for me?

No. Cambree builds the playbook; you run it. It gets you the meeting you deserve and a sharp perspective to walk in with. The rep still has to be good.

Does it replace my reps?

No. It makes a new rep useful in week one instead of month three, and gives a tenured rep the two hours back they spend researching every account.

What if it's wrong?

Every claim is source-tagged so you can check it. When we don't know, we say so instead of inventing. And when an account isn't a fit, we score it that way.
